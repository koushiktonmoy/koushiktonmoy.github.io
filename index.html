Koushik Ahamed Tonmoy

AI Researcher | NLP, Vision-Language Reasoning & AI Evaluation

Dhaka, Bangladesh

koushik.tonmoy [at] northsouth [dot] edu

Hi! I am an undergraduate AI researcher focusing on building rigorous evaluations for reasoning, cultural understanding, and multimodal intelligence, with interests in vision–language reasoning, LLM evaluation, Bengali NLP, and trustworthy AI.

Vision-Language and Geo-Temporal Reasoning. I study how vision-language models understand spatial, temporal, and geographic information. I contributed to TimeSpot, a benchmark for geo-temporal understanding in vision-language models, accepted at ICML 2026. I am also involved in a review of geospatial and geo-temporal reasoning in vision-language and large language models, currently under review at ACL Rolling Review.

Culturally Grounded NLP and LLM Evaluation. I evaluate language models in underrepresented settings, especially Bengali. I contributed to BengaliMoralBench, a benchmark for auditing moral reasoning in Bengali, accepted at ACM FAccT 2026.

Formal and Computational Reasoning. I study whether large language models can turn natural-language instructions into executable systems. My current research tests whether LLMs can design Turing machines from language descriptions and evaluates their structural and functional correctness. This work is currently under review at ACL Rolling Review.

Reliable and Applied Machine Learning. My research also includes transformer-based approaches for source-code vulnerability detection, with a paper accepted at the Muslims in ML Workshop at ICML 2026.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering at North South University, with specialization coursework in machine learning and natural language processing. Alongside my research, I serve as a Marker in the Department of English and Modern Languages, where I support assessment, examination, and academic evaluation.
